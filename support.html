Common questions

My history disappeared

Results are stored in your browser, not on a server. They are lost if you clear site data, use a private window, switch browsers or switch devices. There is no copy anywhere else, so there is nothing we can restore — that is the trade-off for a site with no accounts.

How do I delete everything LampType has stored?

Open the history panel and choose Clear history. That removes your results, your personal bests and your race count immediately. To clear the remaining preference (your theme and mode settings) as well, clear site data for this domain in your browser settings. Nothing is kept anywhere else, so once it is gone from your device it is gone.

My score seems wrong

The formulas are spelled out on the about page. The two that surprise people most: accuracy counts every keystroke you ever made, so a mistake you corrected still costs you; and the space closing a correctly typed word counts toward your WPM.

I can't get out of the typing area with the keyboard

Press shift+tab. Plain tab is reserved for restarting the test, which is the convention typing tests use.

The background is distracting

Switch to any theme other than lava lamp from the picker in the header — paper, ink, linen, oxide and iris have no animated backdrop at all. Turning on reduce motion in your operating system also stills it.

Is there an app?

No, but the site can be installed to your home screen from most mobile browsers and works offline once loaded. It is designed for a physical keyboard.

Reporting a bug

The more of this you can include, the faster it gets fixed:

  • Your browser and operating system, with versions.
  • The mode and settings you were using (for example “1 word, 30s, punctuation on”).
  • What you expected to happen, and what happened instead.
  • Anything red in the browser console, if you know how to open it.

Privacy requests

Because there are no accounts and no server, we hold nothing about you to look up, export or erase — your data is on your own device and the button above deletes it. If you want to exercise a data protection right against the advertising side of the site, the privacy policy explains what is collected and by whom, and email us at the address above if you would like help with it.
