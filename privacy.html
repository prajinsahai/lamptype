The short version. Typing on your own transmits nothing. Every keystroke is processed on your own device, and your results are saved only in your own browser. There are no accounts and no database holding your data.

Racing a friend is the one exception: while you are in a race room, the name you chose, your position in the passage and your final speed and accuracy are sent to the race server so the other racers can see you move. The text you type is not sent, the room exists only in memory, and it is discarded when the last person leaves.

The exception is advertising. This site carries ads served by Google, and Google does set cookies and receive technical data such as your IP address. Most of what follows is about that.

What stays on your device

LampType uses your browser's local storage. This is not a cookie and is never transmitted — it is a small amount of data kept on your machine so the site remembers your settings and results between visits.

KeyWhat it holds
tt:settings Your mode, test length, punctuation and numbers toggles, theme and light/dark preference.
tt:history Your last 50 results: speed, accuracy, consistency, character counts, the mode used and a timestamp. Personal bests are derived from this list rather than stored separately.
tt:races How many races against the AI opponent you have finished, used to calibrate it. Races against other people are not counted here.

None of this is sent anywhere and none of it identifies you. You can erase all of it at any time with Clear history in the history panel, or by clearing site data in your browser.

What we do not collect

  • The text you type. It is compared against the target words in your browser and then discarded. It is never uploaded, logged or stored — a race sends a character count, never the characters.
  • Accounts. There is no sign-up, so no name, email or password.
  • Analytics. This site runs no analytics or tracking scripts of its own.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this site or other sites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to this and other sites on the internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party cookies from participating vendors at aboutads.info and, in Europe, Your Online Choices.
  • How Google handles data from sites that use its services is set out at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We never receive and cannot see the data Google collects. We see only aggregate earnings reports, which say nothing about individual visitors.

Consent, if you are in Europe

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland you will be asked for consent before any advertising cookie is set, through a consent management platform certified by Google under the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework. You may refuse, in which case you will see non-personalised ads, and you can change your answer at any time using the link the consent tool leaves on the page.

The settings and history described above do not depend on that consent. They are strictly necessary for a feature you asked for, they stay on your device, and they are never used for advertising.

Legal bases

Where the UK or EU GDPR applies we rely on consent for advertising and any non-essential storage, and on our legitimate interest in operating and securing the site for the strictly necessary storage that makes the test work.

Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, to withdraw consent, and to complain to your data protection authority.

In practice we hold nothing about you to hand over: there is no account and no server-side record. Your data is on your own device and you delete it yourself with Clear history. For the advertising side, use the Google controls linked above, or write to us and we will help.

Children

LampType is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information through this site, contact us and we will delete it. Where a visitor's age is unknown, ad requests are configured to suppress personalised advertising.

Transfers and retention

Your results do not leave your device except during a race, where your name, position and final figures pass through the race server and are discarded with the room. Google operates globally and may process advertising data outside your country under its own safeguards, described in the policies linked above. Your local data is kept until you delete it; history is capped at the 50 most recent results and older entries fall off automatically.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Because it holds no accounts and no server-side personal data, there is no central store of user data to breach.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will update the date at the top and note significant changes on the site. Continuing to use LampType after a change means you accept the updated policy.
